## Brand & Style
The design system embodies intentional calm, razor-sharp clarity, and frictionless productivity. Tailored for focused professionals, creatives, and intentional thinkers, it strips away cognitive clutter to prioritize chronological orientation and deep work.

The aesthetic fuses Japanese minimal reductionism with modern neo-grotesque precision:
- **Purity over Ornamentation:** Generous negative space acts as a functional pacing mechanism between tasks rather than passive void.
- **Micro-Interactions & Celebration:** Interacting with completed routines yields brief, vivid bursts of energy against an otherwise serene canvas.
- **Architectural Rhythm:** Razor-thin structural borders and disciplined horizontal rules anchor daily schedules without visual heaviness.

## Colors
The palette balances an ultra-clean, warm-monochrome foundation with targeted kinetic accents:
- **Base Surfaces:** Canvas layers are structured using crystalline white (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated cards, layered over warm off-white canvases (`#FAFAFA` and `#F4F4F5`).
- **Typography & Structural Contrast:** Primary text anchors heavily at `#09090B` for effortless legibility in daylight. Secondary metadata, timestamps, and muted labels sit at `#27272A` and `#71717A`.
- **Accent Ignition:** Electric Indigo (`#6366F1`) serves exclusively as the action trigger—used for current-time indicators, primary FABs, active toggle pills, and selected timeline intervals.
- **Completion State:** Tertiary Emerald (`#10B981`) is reserved strictly for completed items and habit streaks, reinforcing a quiet psychological reward.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Micro-borders rely on high-precision neutrals (`#E4E4E7` to `#F4F4F5`) to establish structure without optical noise.

## Typography
Built entirely upon `Geist`, this system leverages engineered monospace-like balance with proportional geometric rhythm.

- **Numerics & Timeline:** Timestamps and duration tags benefit from tabular lining figures, preventing layout jank when schedules refresh or counters tick.
- **Headings:** Headlines demand negative tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.03em`) to mimic editorial Swiss print standards.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Body text never drops below 12px (`0.75rem`), preserving immediate scanability when glancing at daily agendas while in transit.

## Layout & Spacing
A fluid 4-column layout governs mobile environments, evolving to 8 columns on tablets and 12 columns for widescreen planning boards:
- **Vertical Rhythm:** A strict 4px / 8px atomic grid drives scheduling blocks. One hour equates to a standardized 64px vertical interval, while half-hour increments align precisely to 32px.
- **Margins & Gutter Logic:** Mobile viewports use 16px (`1rem`) outer margins to maximize interactive surface space, expanding to 32px on larger screens to avoid overstretched reading spans.
- **Breathing Space:** Elements never crowd task titles; list items maintain minimum internal padding of `space-md` (`1rem`) vertically to ensure effortless touch targeting (minimum 44x44pt).

## Elevation & Depth
The system renounces heavy artificial drop shadows in favor of **low-contrast micro-outlines, structural boundaries, and subtle surface layering**:
- **Baseline Canvas (Level 0):** Neutral `#FAFAFA` canvas with zero shadow.
- **Structural Elevation (Level 1 - Task Cards & Modules):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface bordered by a crisp `1px` stroke of `#E4E4E7`. Ambient drop shadow is kept whisper-quiet: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(9, 9, 11, 0.03)`.
- **Floating Modals & Sheets (Level 2):** Floating creation sheets use an ambient veil: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(9, 9, 11, 0.06), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(9, 9, 11, 0.04)` with a micro-border of `#E4E4E7`.
- **Timehead Scrim:** The active timeline tracker features a razor-sharp 1.5px solid line in `#6366F1` with an ultra-subtle diffuse pulse glow (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.15)`).

## Shapes
A balanced roundedness architecture harmonizes structural cards with tactile pills:
- **Outer Shells & Containers:** Base task cards, modal sheets, and time-block regions use `rounded-lg` (16px / `1rem`), projecting a welcoming yet structured feel.
- **Interactive Tags & Micro-Badges:** Filter chips, category indicators, time badges, and status pills strictly employ continuous full-radius pill styling (`rounded-full` / 9999px).
- **Checkpoints:** Habit checkboxes and item controls use `rounded` (8px / `0.5rem`) for a softer, organic interactive feel.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action Button:** Solid `#6366F1` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded-full`, 48px height for thumb-friendly reach. Active state drops opacity to `0.9` with a subtle scale transform (`0.98`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** `#FFFFFF` background with `1px solid #E4E4E7`, `#09090B` text. Transitions to `#F4F4F5` on tap.

### Chips & Pill Badges
- **Category & Duration Pills:** Rendered with full-pill contours (`rounded-full`), `space-xs` vertical by `space-sm` horizontal padding. Neutral state: `#F4F4F5` background with `#27272A` text. Selected state: `#6366F1` background with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Task Lists & Schedule Items
- **List Rows:** Set against `#FFFFFF` background with a subtle bottom divider (`1px solid #F4F4F5`). Tapping triggers an instant slate-50 active press state.
- **Strikethrough Dynamic:** Checking an item transitions text color to `#A1A1AA` with an animated strike line, accompanied by a discreet vibration pattern and emerald border accent.

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls
- **Circular Check Targets:** 22px diameter circles. Unchecked: `1.5px solid #D4D4D8` border with a transparent fill. Checked: `#10B981` solid fill containing an inset white SVG checkmark.

### Input Fields & Quick-Add
- **Inline Task Creator:** Clean borderless baseline with a persistent `1px solid #E4E4E7` underline that expands into a 2px `#6366F1` border on focus. Typography sits at `body-lg` (`1rem`) to avoid auto-zoom on mobile web runtimes.

### Schedule Cards & Time Blocks
- **Focus Blocks:** Inset cards utilizing 16px internal padding, `rounded-lg`, framed by a `1px solid #E4E4E7` boundary. Left border features an optional 3px solid category color accent bar.
